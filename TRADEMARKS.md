# Project names and branding

Havebee is the project's public name. Existing builds, identifiers and data
folders currently use its earlier name, Hive. This policy concerns these names
and the project's honeycomb artwork as identifiers of this project. It makes
no claim over unrelated uses of the word "Hive" and does not assert registered
trademark status.

The code license does not grant trademark rights. Luciano Sánchez controls
the branding of the official project, subject to applicable law and third-party
rights.

## Identifying the project

You may truthfully refer to the project in articles, tutorials, reviews,
compatibility statements and attribution. You may describe an independent fork
as "based on Havebee" when the relationship is clear.

Redistributors of unmodified copies should preserve attribution and clearly
identify themselves as the distributor. Do not describe an independent build,
service, account or release as official, endorsed or maintained by the project
unless the maintainer has authorized that representation.

Modified forks should use their own prominent product name and identity so
users can distinguish them from the official project. Keep the original
copyright and license notices required by the applicable licenses. Contact the
maintainer through the canonical repository for a proposed use that could
imply sponsorship or affiliation.

This policy does not restrict rights granted by the source-code licenses,
prohibit commercial use permitted by those licenses, or limit lawful
descriptive, nominative or fair use of names and marks. Copyright permissions
for artwork and trademark rights are separate matters.
